Homebuyer Education

HUD-style classes, 1:1 counseling, education lock-in, and certificate issuance. HUD-9902 compliant reporting built in. Electronic signatures. One console your counselors, educators, and directors all use — the front door to every assistance program.

What this module does

The HBE Console delivers the full housing counseling lifecycle — intake, financial assessment, education classes, 1:1 counseling, and HUD-9902 ARM reporting. Clients apply online and sign electronically. Staff schedule in-person and virtual classes with capacity management. Attendance is tracked per person, certificates issue automatically, and every step is auditable.

Who it’s for

HUD-approved housing counseling agencies, nonprofit homebuyer education providers, and any organization required to deliver pre-purchase education before disbursing assistance funds.

Why it matters for affordable housing: Most state and federal down payment programs — including Florida SHIP and HUD HOME — require borrowers to complete a HUD-certified homebuyer education workshop before closing. Homebuyer Education is the system of record that proves it.

Connected to: The same contact record flows into Down Payment Assistance, so a household’s class completion is one click from their loan application.

Built on

Microsoft Power Platform — Dataverse for the data and security model, model-driven apps for staff, Power Automate for workflows, Power Pages for constituent self-service, and Copilot Studio agents wired through the native Dataverse MCP server so frontier AI models can read and act on the same records the staff are looking at.
